welcome

Botanical hair oils rooted in nature, made for everyday care. Softness, shine, and scalp comfort without heavy chemicals.

Zulfaan Botanics started in Bahawalpur with a simple idea: honest botanical hair oils you can use at home. Our blends draw from traditional oiling rituals and everyday ingredients like almond, amla, kulwanji, and black seed. Each bottle is made to nourish the scalp, soften dry lengths, and leave hair looking healthy without a heavy finish.
